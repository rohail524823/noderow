# NodeRow — keyword and topic universe

Part 7.1 of the build brief. Built 2026-08-28 from Google autocomplete (real query
strings, not estimates), GoHighLevel's own help centre and changelog, and the
competitive analysis in `STRATEGY.md`.

**Volumes are deliberately absent.** No keyword tool was used, so any number here
would be invented. Clusters are ranked by *observed demand signals* (autocomplete
depth, competitor coverage gaps, commercial intent) rather than fabricated volume.
Google Search Console becomes the real keyword tool once the site has impressions.

---

## The headline finding

**`gohighlevel cost calculator` is a live Google autocomplete suggestion.**

So is `gohighlevel cost per sms`, `gohighlevel cost per text`, `gohighlevel cost
per email`, `gohighlevel hidden costs` and `gohighlevel hidden fees`.

There is direct, demonstrated search demand for the exact asset already built — and
the one competitor claiming a calculator (`scalemyghl.com/pricing-calculator`) has
no calculator on the page. This is the single clearest unoccupied position in the
niche, and NodeRow now holds it with primary-sourced rates.

## Deliberately skipped

- **Definitional "what is gohighlevel"** — ranks at position 34–58 with near-zero
  clicks per the brief. `is gohighlevel a crm` is borderline; answer it inside the
  review rather than as a standalone page.
- **`gohighlevel 30 day trial`** — real demand, but the trial is 14 days on the
  pricing page and offering incentives needs written authorisation. Do not chase it.
- **`gohighlevel login`** — navigational, worthless to us.
- Certifications, courses, monitoring guides — other properties.

## Cadence

One page per day maximum. Publishing faster builds a backlog Google discovers and
declines to crawl.

## Method note

Autocomplete reflects query *language*, not volume. Treat this ordering as a
hypothesis to be corrected by Search Console within the first 60 days of
impressions. Any page here that turns out to attract impressions with no clicks
should be rewritten or retired rather than defended.
