Agency / CRM platform

HighLevel Inc. Pays commission

GoHighLevel

Best for: Agencies and service businesses that want CRM, pipelines, email/SMS, booking, funnels and reporting billed as one product instead of six.

Weakness: It is a platform, not a connector. If your work is moving data between arbitrary APIs, GoHighLevel is the wrong shape and you will end up bolting Make or n8n onto it anyway.

40% recurring, plus 5% second tier. The dashboard states no fixed term — it is not capped at 12 months the way Make and n8n are.

Visit GoHighLevel

General automation / iPaaS

Zapier Inc. No commission

Zapier

Best for: Teams who need the widest app catalogue in the industry and whose volume is low enough that per-task pricing never bites.

Weakness: Cost at volume. Per-task pricing that is fine at 1,000 runs is punishing at 100,000.

No public cash affiliate program. NodeRow earns nothing from Zapier, ever.

Visit Zapier

Celonis Pays commission

Make

Best for: Visual multi-step workflows with branching, where per-operation pricing beats Zapier's per-task pricing at volume.

Weakness: Operations are counted per module run, so a loop over 500 rows is 500 operations. Easy to underestimate.

35% for 12 months. Clock starts at registration, not first payment.

Visit Make

n8n GmbH Pays commission

n8n Cloud

Best for: Teams who want per-execution rather than per-step pricing, and the option to drop into code when the visual nodes run out.

Weakness: Steeper learning curve than Make or Zapier. Expression syntax and item-vs-batch handling are where new users lose a day.

30% for 12 months, 90-day cookie. Cloud only — self-hosted pays nothing.

Visit n8n Cloud

n8n GmbH No commission

n8n self-hosted

Best for: Anyone comfortable running a container who wants unlimited executions for the price of a small VPS.

Weakness: You own uptime, upgrades, backups and credential security. That is real work, not zero.

Self-hosted n8n pays no commission. NodeRow earns nothing when you self-host.

Visit n8n self-hosted
